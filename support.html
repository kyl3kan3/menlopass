peri Support

Help for the private menopause tracker.

Getting started

The first page is a letter with blanks: what has been hardest, where your periods are, your age, and whether an appointment is coming. After that, every day is a page. Tap Log to file what happens with the time (hot flash, night sweat, woke, and the words you chose), or open the app between 10 pm and 6 am for the night desk. Close the day with one word and four checkboxes. The Dossier gathers the findings, your treatments, the questions and the opening statement for the appointment, and exports a one-page brief. The Index holds the reference chapters and tools, found by question. Treatments, lab results and after-visit plans are managed from the Dossier.

Backups and device changes

Your data stays on the device. Before changing or resetting an iPhone or iPad, open You (the person icon on the masthead), choose Export or restore, and save a password-protected encrypted backup. Keep its password somewhere safe because peri cannot recover it. On the new installation, choose that .menocompass file and enter the same password. Plain JSON remains available as an unencrypted compatibility export.

Face ID, reminders, Apple Health, and widgets

Open You to turn on App Lock, set optional daily or weekly reminders, and connect Apple Health. App Lock requires Face ID or Touch ID to be enrolled on the device. Notification permission is requested only when you save an enabled reminder; if previously denied, re-enable it in iOS Settings. Apple Health sync is read-only and user initiated. Missing values can mean no matching data or that a read type was not shared. Add or remove peri widgets from the iOS Home Screen; widget taps open the log sheet or the Dossier.

Reset or delete

Open You. Redo the first page asks the letter’s questions again while keeping your pages, treatments, labs and dossier. Delete everything permanently erases all local information after a separate confirmation. peri does not create an online account. Deleting app data does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

Subscriptions

On iPhone and iPad, peri requires an active monthly or annual subscription before app features are available. There is no free tier or free trial. The exact price and billing period are shown on the subscription screen before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings. Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling the app or moving to another device with the same Apple ID. A successful restore does not restore locally stored health entries; use a password-protected peri backup for those.

Troubleshooting

In the browser/PWA version, if entries are not surviving a restart, check whether private browsing or storage restrictions are enabled; the app warns when it has fallen back to temporary in-memory storage. In the native app, if App Lock, reminders, Apple Health, or widgets are unavailable, confirm you installed the latest TestFlight/App Store build rather than opening the project in Expo Go.

Safety

peri is educational and is not emergency or diagnostic care. Use the in-app urgent guidance for warning signs, and contact local emergency services for an emergency.
