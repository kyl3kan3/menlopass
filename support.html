MenoCompass Support

Help for the private menopause tracker.

Getting started

Complete the optional profile, then use Today to record only what changed. Add medications and lab results from Meds. Reports can be printed or saved from the Report tab.

Backups and device changes

Your data stays on the device. Before changing or resetting a device, open Settings and export a JSON backup. Restore that file from Settings on the new installation.

Subscriptions

On iPhone and iPad, MenoCompass Pro unlocks advanced 30- and 90-day trends and clinician-ready reports. Use Explore Pro to view monthly and annual plans. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings. Use Restore after reinstalling the app or moving to another device with the same Apple ID. A successful restore does not restore locally stored health entries; use a JSON backup for those.

Troubleshooting

If entries are not surviving a restart, check whether private browsing or storage restrictions are enabled. The app warns when it has fallen back to temporary in-memory storage.

Safety

MenoCompass is educational and is not emergency or diagnostic care. Use the in-app urgent guidance for warning signs, and contact local emergency services for an emergency.
