MenoCompass Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

MenoCompass is a private, offline-first symptom and treatment tracker. The app does not require an account and does not operate a server-side database.

Data stored by the app

Information you enter—including symptoms, medications, lab results, notes, profile details, questionnaire scores, and screening dates—is stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive or have access to this information.

Diagnostics and advertising attribution

The native app uses EAS Observe for app-performance diagnostics and AppsFlyer and the Meta App Events SDK to measure app installs, launches, advertising attribution, and visits to the subscription screen. These services may process app version, device model, operating-system version, coarse network information such as IP address, and anonymous installation or SDK identifiers. On iOS, MenoCompass requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before enabling access to the advertising identifier. You may decline without losing any app or subscription feature.

MenoCompass does not send symptoms, medications, lab results, notes, profile answers, questionnaire scores, screening dates, clinician reports, or exported files to Expo, AppsFlyer, Meta, RevenueCat, or the developer. It does not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, or free-text entries to analytics or advertising services. The app does not display third-party ads or sell personal data.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

The native app uses RevenueCat to check subscription status, validate purchases, and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives App Store purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier for app functionality and subscription analytics. When advertising measurement is permitted, MenoCompass may also provide RevenueCat with AppsFlyer, Meta, and device attribution identifiers so RevenueCat can report subscription lifecycle events—such as starts, renewals, and cancellations—to those services without recording the same purchase twice. Apple processes App Store payments under Apple’s privacy policy.

Service providers

Limited diagnostic and attribution data is processed under the privacy terms of Expo, AppsFlyer, Meta, and RevenueCat. The browser-based web app does not initialize the native AppsFlyer, Meta, RevenueCat, or EAS Observe SDKs.

Exports

If you export a backup, CSV, PDF, or clinician report, you control where that file is stored or sent. Exported files may contain sensitive health information and are not encrypted by MenoCompass.

Deleting your data

You can erase all app data from Settings. Removing the app or clearing its local storage also removes the device-local copy. This cannot be undone unless you previously created a backup.

Medical information

MenoCompass provides general education and self-tracking. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace care from a qualified clinician.
