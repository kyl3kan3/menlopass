MenoCompass Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

MenoCompass is a private, offline-first symptom and treatment tracker. The app does not require an account and does not operate a server-side database.

Data stored by the app

Information you enter—including symptoms, medications, treatment follow-ups, appointment questions and plans, lab results, notes, profile details, questionnaire scores, and screening dates—is stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive or have access to this information. In the native iPhone and iPad app, the health record is stored in an encrypted file using a device-bound key kept in the iOS Keychain and is not copied into WebView browser storage. The browser/PWA version uses browser local storage, which MenoCompass does not encrypt.

Apple Health

Apple Health connection is optional, read-only, and begins only when you select Connect Apple Health or Sync Apple Health. MenoCompass requests read access to step count, sleep analysis, and body weight. It saves only the displayed aggregate step and sleep summary and latest body-weight value in your device-local MenoCompass record. It does not write to Apple Health, read data in the background, store or expose raw samples or source/device metadata, or send Apple Health data to the developer, analytics, attribution, advertising, or subscription providers. Raw sleep samples are processed transiently on the device to calculate the displayed aggregate. Apple may not disclose whether an individual read type was denied, so unavailable values are not treated as zero. You can change Health access in iOS Settings and remove the saved summary from MenoCompass at any time.

App Lock, reminders, and widgets

Optional App Lock uses Apple’s local authentication interface for Face ID, Touch ID, or device-passcode fallback; MenoCompass does not receive or store biometric data. Reminder preferences and notification identifiers are stored in the iOS Keychain. Notifications are scheduled locally, use generic wording, and contain no symptoms, treatment names, or other health details. Permission is requested only when you enable and save a reminder. The Home Screen widgets receive only whether today’s check-in is complete and how many of the last seven days are confirmed. They do not receive symptom values, medications, labs, notes, profile details, or Apple Health values.

Diagnostics and advertising attribution

The native app uses EAS Observe for app-performance diagnostics, privacy-safe product events, and sanitized JavaScript error reports, and uses AppsFlyer, the Meta App Events SDK, and the TikTok App Events SDK to measure app installs, launches, advertising attribution, retention, and visits to the subscription screen. These services may process app version, device model, operating-system version, coarse network information such as IP address, and anonymous installation or SDK identifiers. MenoCompass replaces original error messages before automatic or explicitly handled reports send an error name and stack location to EAS Observe. EAS Observe does not capture native crashes. On iOS, MenoCompass requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before enabling access to the advertising identifier. You may decline without losing any app or subscription feature.

MenoCompass does not send symptoms, medications, lab results, notes, profile answers, questionnaire scores, screening dates, clinician reports, or exported files to Expo, AppsFlyer, Meta, TikTok, RevenueCat, or the developer. It does not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, or free-text entries to analytics, diagnostics, or advertising services. The app does not display third-party ads or sell personal data.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

The native app requires an active monthly or annual subscription and does not provide a free tier or free trial. It uses RevenueCat to check subscription status, validate purchases, and restore purchases before app content is shown. RevenueCat receives App Store purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier for app functionality and subscription analytics. When advertising measurement is permitted, MenoCompass may also provide RevenueCat with AppsFlyer, Meta, and device attribution identifiers so RevenueCat can report subscription lifecycle events—such as starts, renewals, and cancellations—to those services without recording the same purchase twice. Apple processes App Store payments under Apple’s privacy policy.

Service providers

Limited diagnostic and attribution data is processed under the privacy terms of Expo, AppsFlyer, Meta, TikTok, and RevenueCat. The browser-based health tracker does not initialize the native AppsFlyer, Meta, TikTok, RevenueCat, or EAS Observe SDKs. The separate public App Store download page contains no health-entry fields and does not load advertising pixels; if you select its download button, your browser opens an AppsFlyer OneLink attribution URL before the App Store. AppsFlyer may receive ordinary request information such as IP address, browser details, the referring campaign parameters, and the fact that the link was selected. No symptom, medication, note, profile, or other health-entry data is included.

Exports

If you export a backup, CSV, PDF, or clinician report, you control where that file is stored or sent. The native app can create a portable .menocompass backup encrypted with a password you choose; that password is never stored or recoverable by the developer. Plain JSON, CSV, and PDF exports may contain sensitive health information and are not encrypted by MenoCompass, so save or share them only with services and people you trust.

Resetting onboarding or deleting your data

From Profile under Account & data, Reset onboarding restarts setup without deleting your profile answers, health logs, treatments, labs, or reports. Delete app profile & data permanently erases the complete device-local copy after a separate confirmation. Removing the app or clearing its local storage also removes the local copy. Deletion cannot be undone unless you previously created a backup, and it does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Medical information

MenoCompass provides general education and self-tracking. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace care from a qualified clinician.
