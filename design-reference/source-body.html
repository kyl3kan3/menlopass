Twilight — a lit instrument, not a wellness app.

The redesign drops the warm-cream / serif / terracotta look for a dark-first clinical identity: deep teal-ink grounds, one warm amber-gold signal rationed to states and dose-change markers, severity encoded as four weights of that single hue, Bricolage Grotesque carrying the display voice. It leans into the 3 a.m. night-sweat log the product is built around. Both modes are first-class — the last screen shows daytime light. Tap the severity dots to feel the check-in.
