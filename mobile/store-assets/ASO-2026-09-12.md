# peri: US App Store ASO

Prepared September 12, 2026. Implementation is in `mobile/store.config.json`; research responses are preserved in `aso-research-2026-09-12.json`. These are prepared release assets, not a claim that App Store Connect has been updated.

## Metadata decision

- Device display name: **peri**.
- Store title: **peri: Menopause Tracker** (23 characters).
- Subtitle: **Perimenopause & Symptom Log** (27 characters).
- Keyword field: 100 ASCII bytes, with no repeated title/subtitle terms. Secondary terms are relevant feature hypotheses, not individually measured opportunities.
- Description leads with symptoms, treatment context, and appointment preparation. Subscription terms and privacy/medical limitations remain explicit.

These are vendor scores, not search counts, install forecasts, or proof of a future ranking. All six responses report 210 apps, so app counts do not differentiate these opportunities. The existing listing already covered the strongest category terms; the improvement is a shorter brand, clearer positioning, fuller secondary coverage, and more direct conversion copy.

The perimenopause deep dive ranked [Menelle](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6757102221) first, [Lila](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6667108028) second, and [Health & Her](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1519199698) third. Category discovery also surfaced HRTMe and Menopause Symptoms Tracker. Category and symptom language is competitive; peri's proposed differentiation is a private record that connects symptoms, treatment changes, and appointment reports. Competitor positioning is not evidence that those features are unique to peri. Brand availability has not been established by this keyword research.

## Release and measurement

The store version now matches the app's 1.2.1 version. A new native build is required for the device name, permission text, and shortcuts. Keep bundle/package IDs, EAS project, URL schemes, storage keys, encrypted backup formats, and RevenueCat entitlement `MenoCompass Pro` unchanged to preserve continuity. The hosted RevenueCat paywall/product display labels still need review for legacy branding before release. Existing URLs remain valid support destinations.

Upload the prepared metadata and screenshots to the editable iOS version, confirm name acceptance and native-device behavior, and submit through the normal release process. This change does not upload metadata, submit a binary, or publish a release.

Before launch, record the previous 28 days of US App Store Search impressions, product-page views, first-time downloads, and paid subscription starts. Compare the next 28 days by the same source and territory, annotating ad spend and release dates. Monitor the six researched queries weekly. Keep organic search separate from paid acquisition; use paid starts per first-time download as a guardrail against attracting low-intent traffic. No numeric uplift target is supportable without the baseline.

After establishing the new listing baseline, test a screenshot variant leading with appointment preparation against the tracking-first creative through Apple's product page optimization. Change one creative variable, use Apple's reported confidence, and avoid declaring a winner from a handful of downloads.
